## Research and routing notes

The [City of Naperville historic building manual](https://www.naperville.il.us/contentassets/7fed1bf2ba19496fa9a037f019616748/cdg-historic-building-design-and-resource-manual.pdf), section C.8, and [Ballard Historical Society](https://www.ballardhistory.org/style-summary-american-foursquare/) support the form, common plan, roof, porch, and stylistic overlap. Oregon's [Irvington Historic District record](https://heritagedata.prd.state.or.us/historic/index.cfm?do=v.dsp_siteSummary&resultDisplay=657321) explicitly inventories 305 Foursquare buildings and identifies the McBride House at 2401 NE 23rd Avenue. The [Sears catalog archive](https://www.usmodernist.org/index-sears.htm) documents mail-order housing generally; the article does not claim that any illustrated or named house was a Sears kit. Renovation guidance is framed by the [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/guidelines-rehabilitating-historic-buildings.htm) and remains building-specific design advice.
