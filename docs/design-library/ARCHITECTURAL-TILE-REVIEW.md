## Source notes

The Tile Council of North America confirms porcelain's 0.5% or lower water absorption definition and that product-specific use ratings still matter. The article also distinguishes finished tile and grout from a shower waterproofing assembly. Its source list links to TCNA and Daltile guidance. Verify the selected product and current installation methods before specifying a project.
