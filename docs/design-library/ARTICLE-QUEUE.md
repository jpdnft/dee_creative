## Review record template
For each article, record:
- Article / slug / category
- Current status
- Draft location and preview route
- Research/source verification notes
- Image status (assets vs. briefs/placeholders)
- Reviewer comments and outstanding issues
- Content approval date (explicit)
- Publication authorization/date (explicit, separate)

## Workflow guardrail
Work on only one article at a time. After completing a draft or revisions, stop and wait. Never advance to another article, mark approved, merge to production, or deploy merely because previous work is complete.
