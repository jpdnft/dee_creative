The article adapts `docs/CAPE-COD-HOUSE.md` into the House Types & Forms template, with nine body sections and five supplied illustrations. A labeled SVG elevation and selectable At a glance text distinguish half, three-quarter, and full Cape arrangements. The diagram is conceptual, not a measured historic elevation.

No supplied exterior clearly shows the four-bay three-quarter Cape, so the article teaches that form in the diagram and text without mislabeling a photograph. The Washington State Department of Archaeology and Historic Preservation supports the Northwest Revival chronology and the 1939 Western Pine Association model-home claim. Captions identify the supplied images as illustrations, not documented houses.
