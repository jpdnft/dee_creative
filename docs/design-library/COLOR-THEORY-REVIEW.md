## Source notes

Josef Albers's *Interaction of Color* supports the context discussion. The U.S. Department of Energy and paint manufacturers provide guidance on light, undertones, and sample testing. Color schemes and 60–30–10 are presented as optional tools rather than formulas. Physical samples in the real room remain the practical test.
