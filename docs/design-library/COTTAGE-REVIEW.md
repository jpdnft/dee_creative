The article now cites Historic England for the picturesque cottage and house–garden tradition, National Park Service documentation for American Storybook revival, and City of Portland materials for the local English Cottage examples in King's Hill and Irvington. The regional copy was tightened around these records. Captions identify the images as illustrations, not documentary photographs.
