[The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/fr/essays/design-1975-present) provides context for later postmodern experimentation.
- [The Oregon Encyclopedia](https://www.oregonencyclopedia.org/articles/reuse-and-restore-movement/) and the [City of Portland's salvage guide](https://www.portland.gov/bps/resourcefulpdx/home-improvement/salvage-and-reuse) support the local reuse discussion. The city's [Irvington page](https://www.portland.gov/neighborhoods/northeast/irvington) supports the historic-house context.
- [National Park Service Preservation Brief 18](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/upload/preservation-brief-18-interiors.pdf), [Portland's permit guide](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/do-you-need-permit/residential-permits), and [Portland's lead and asbestos guidance](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/asbestos-and-lead-based-paint) support the renovation cautions.
