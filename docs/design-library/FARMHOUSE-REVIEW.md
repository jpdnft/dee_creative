The article adapts `FARMHOUSE.md` into the House Types & Forms template, with nine body sections and five supplied illustrations. A labeled SVG farmstead sketch and selectable At a glance text show the house's relationship to its work yard, garden, barn, and fields. The sketch is conceptual, not a measured historic site plan.

The draft identifies farmhouse as a building's agricultural use and context, while Modern Farmhouse describes a contemporary design vocabulary. The Oregon examples are supported by state historic-site records; captions do not imply the supplied images document those properties. The supplied draft's comparison table was adapted into accessible prose for the article template.
