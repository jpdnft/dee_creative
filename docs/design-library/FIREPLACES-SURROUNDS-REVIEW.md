## Source check

- The [National Park Service interior preservation brief](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/upload/preservation-brief-18-interiors.pdf) supports identifying historic interior features before altering them.
- [Historic England](https://historicengland.org.uk/advice/your-home/improvement/fireplace/) discusses the character and retention of historic fireplaces.
- [Portland's stove, insert, and chimney guidance](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/stoves-inserts-chimneys) and [residential mechanical permit page](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/residential-mechanical-permits) support the local permitting discussion. Confirm requirements for each actual project.
- [Oregon DEQ](https://www.oregon.gov/deq/Residential/Pages/woodstovesFAQ.aspx) and the [EPA appliance FAQ](https://www.epa.gov/burnwise/frequent-questions-about-wood-burning-appliances) provide background on wood-burning appliances. The EPA page is a historical record that is no longer updated; the article does not use it for current permitting claims.
