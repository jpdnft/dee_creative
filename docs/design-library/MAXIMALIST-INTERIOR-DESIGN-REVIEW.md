## Research check

- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Rococo Revival parlor](https://www.metmuseum.org/es/about-the-met/collection-areas/the-american-wing/period-rooms/rococo-revival-parlor) documents nineteenth-century patterned surfaces and collecting. The article treats this as one influence, not a direct origin story.
- [Cooper Hewitt](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2012/11/21/dorothy-drapers-rhododendron/) supports the passage on Dorothy Draper's vivid color and decorative scale.
- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art's design essay](https://www.metmuseum.org/fr/essays/design-1975-present) dates the Memphis group's formation around Ettore Sottsass to 1981.
- [National Park Service Preservation Brief 18](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/upload/preservation-brief-18-interiors.pdf) and the [City of Portland permit guide](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/do-you-need-permit/residential-permits) support the renovation and scope-dependent permit notes.
