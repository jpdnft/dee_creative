## Source and editorial notes

The [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/prsf/learn/historyculture/mediterranean-italian-renaissance-revival.htm) and [City of Miami Beach](https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/architecture/mediterranean-revival/) support the revival context and visible characteristics. Portland's [South Portland district guidelines](https://efiles.portlandoregon.gov/Record/15729425/File/Document) and the [ODOT historic resources report](https://www.i5rosequarter.org/pdfs/environmental_assessment/I5RQ_Historic-Resources-Technical-Report_010819.pdf) support the named local examples. The ODOT URL in the supplied draft no longer resolves; the article uses the available project report instead. The Alameda house is attributed to a [local architectural-history source](https://alamedahistory.org/2009/07/08/style-points-the-mediterranean/), rather than presented as an independently verified official designation.

Planned related materials articles are omitted until their routes exist. Tudor Revival, Colonial Revival, and Contemporary are linked in the private preview. The article footer uses the established Start a Project CTA.
