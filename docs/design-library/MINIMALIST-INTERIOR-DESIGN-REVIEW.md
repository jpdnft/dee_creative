## Research check

- [MoMA's Minimalism entry](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/minimalism) describes the specific American art movement of the 1960s. Its [de Stijl entry](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/de-stijl) documents an earlier, distinct abstract movement.
- [Victoria and Albert Museum](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/what-was-modernism) supports the early modernist discussion of function, simple lines, and reduced applied ornament.
- [Design Museum](https://designmuseum.org/designers/john-pawson) documents Pawson's later architectural exploration of simplicity. The article avoids treating historical Japanese design as one uniform minimalist style.
- [City of Portland](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/do-you-need-permit/residential-permits) supports the article's scope-dependent permit note for home remodeling.
