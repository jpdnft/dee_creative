## Source check

- The [U.S. Geological Survey](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/dimension-stone-statistics-and-information) defines dimension stone and identifies its principal rock families.
- The [Natural Stone Institute's stone-sleuth guide](https://usenaturalstone.org/how-to-be-your-own-stone-sleuth/) distinguishes hardness, acid sensitivity, and porosity and recommends testing actual samples with supplier cooperation.
- [Arizona Tile's natural-stone care guidance](https://www.arizonatile.com/resources/care-maintenance/care-maintenance-for-natural-stone/) informs the maintenance discussion. Product-specific instructions still govern actual installations.

Editorial review should focus on whether the image sequence gives a clear comparison and whether the care and application guidance feels balanced.
