## Source and editorial notes

The article distinguishes physical plaster systems from limewash and Portola's Roman Clay. It treats wet-area suitability as a question for a specified assembly and manufacturer, and does not imply that a protective topcoat makes a decorative coating waterproof. The National Park Service historic plaster brief and Portola's current Roman Clay and Lime Wash guidance were checked during assembly; source links are embedded in the article references. Review the final product-specific guidance before specification.
