## Source and editorial notes

The [National Park Service's ranch interior study](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/identifying-the-1950s-ranch-house-interior-as-a-cultural-resource.htm) supports the range of postwar floor plans and public/private zoning. [Save Our Heritage Organisation](https://www.sohosandiego.org/reflections/2001-3/ranchhouse.htm) supports Cliff May and early California ranch characteristics. Oregon's [Redmond historic resources document](https://www.oregon.gov/oprd/OH/Documents/OR_DeschutesCo_HistoricResidentialResourcesOfRedmond%2COregonMPD.pdf) supports the bungalow-to-ranch outdoor-living shift, and the [Oregon City survey](https://oregon-city.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?clip_id=655&meta_id=31497&view_id=6) distinguishes Early Ranch and Ranch variants. [Restore Oregon](https://restoreoregon.org/2021/06/29/east-portlands-mid-century-modern-neighborhoods/) documents custom ranches in Glendoveer.
