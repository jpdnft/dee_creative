## Source notes

The National Kitchen & Bath Association's kitchen guidelines and U.S. Access Board standards support the planning examples within their respective scopes; ADA standards are not universal rules for private homes. Patrice Foutakis's published archaeological study supports the caution against treating the golden ratio as a universal historical formula. The article's own design advice remains subordinate to actual room measurements and project-specific requirements.
