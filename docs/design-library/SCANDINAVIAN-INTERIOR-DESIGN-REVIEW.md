## Research check

- [ArkDes](https://arkdes.se/en/the-collection/themes/collection-exhibition-2024-2025/the-stockholm-exhibition-1930/) documents the 1930 Stockholm Exhibition and its role in Swedish functionalism.
- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/es/press-releases/schedule-of-exhibitions-septemberdecember-2000-2000-exhibitions) describes the softer lines and natural materials of Scandinavian design by Aalto and Mathsson.
- [Milwaukee Art Museum](https://mam.org/exhibitions/scandinavian-design/) and [Nationalmuseum Sweden](https://www.nationalmuseum.se/en/scandinavian-design-och-usa-1890-1980) document reciprocal Nordic and American design exchange; Nationalmuseum explains the 1950s category name's broad use.
- [Milwaukee Art Museum Research Center](https://blog.mam.org/2023/03/15/paging-through-the-publications-on-view-in-scandinavian-design-and-the-united-states/) confirms that *Design in Scandinavia* toured 24 North American venues in 1954–57.
