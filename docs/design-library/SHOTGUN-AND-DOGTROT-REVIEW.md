# Shotgun and Dogtrot House — editorial review

Status: private draft awaiting editorial review. Publication remains on hold.

Preview: `http://127.0.0.1:3002/design-library/house-types-and-forms/shotgun-dogtrot-house`

The article adapts `SHOTGUN-AND-DOGTROT.md` into the House Types & Forms template, with eight body sections and five supplied illustrations. A labeled SVG plan sketch and selectable At a glance text show the key distinction: a shotgun's route goes through rooms; a dogtrot's route goes between enclosed halves through an open, roofed passage. The sketch is conceptual, not a measured historic plan.

The draft treats the shotgun's deeper ancestry as debated and rejects the street-frontage tax story as an established explanation. The dogtrot example and construction dates follow the National Park Service record. The source images are conceptual illustrations; their captions do not claim documentary provenance.
