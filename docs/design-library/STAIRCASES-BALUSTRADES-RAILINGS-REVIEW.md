## Source check

- [Portland's stair permit guide](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/home-projects/stairs) states that building, replacing, or changing a residential stairway requires a permit and gives guidance on stairs, handrails, guards, and landings.
- [Oregon Building Codes Division](https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/pages/residential-structures.aspx) states that 2026 Residential Specialty Code administrative provisions became mandatory October 1, 2026; construction provisions have a phase-in period until April 1, 2027.
- The [Stairbuilders and Manufacturers Association glossary](https://stairways.org/educate/technical-resources/glossary/) supports the component terminology and the distinction between a balustrade and guard.

Editorial review should focus on whether the six-image sequence gives enough visual variety and whether the renovation and safety discussion feels proportionate to the design content.
