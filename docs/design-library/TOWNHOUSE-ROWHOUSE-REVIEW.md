# Townhouse and Rowhouse — editorial review

Status: draft for private review. Publication remains on hold.

Preview: `http://127.0.0.1:3002/design-library/house-types-and-forms/townhouse-rowhouse`

The article adapts `TOWNHOUSE-ROWHOUSE.md` into the House Types & Forms template. It has nine body sections and five supplied illustrations. The source PNGs remain in `assets/design-library/home-types/townhouse/`; matching WebP derivatives serve the preview.

The draft's Trenkmann Houses example was removed: the National Register nomination documents eight separate contiguous houses, not a verified attached row. The Oregon Historic Sites Database documents the Campbell Townhouses as six brick units built in 1893. The article defines brownstone as a sandstone material, not a house type, and treats *townhouse* and *rowhouse* as usage terms rather than rigid universal categories. Image captions identify conceptual scenes instead of claiming documentary provenance.
