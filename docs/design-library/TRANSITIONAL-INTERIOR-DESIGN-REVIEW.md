## Editorial source check

- [Architectural Digest's designer interviews on transitional design](https://www.architecturaldigest.com/story/transitional-design-101) support the broad definition, the range of interpretations, and the distinction from contemporary interiors. They do not establish a precise founding date; the article avoids one.
- [National Park Service Preservation Brief 18](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/upload/preservation-brief-18-interiors.pdf) supports the renovation advice to identify character-defining spaces, details, and finishes before alteration. The Portland examples and palette suggestions are editorial applications, not claims about a specific documented property.
