## Source notes

U.S. Department of Energy guidance supports the discussion of well-fitted cellular shades and window comfort. U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission guidance supports the cordless-covering recommendation around children. Fabric fullness, mounting, and ordering dimensions remain design guidance to confirm with the chosen manufacturer and installer.
