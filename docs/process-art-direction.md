# Process page visual refinement

The five stages form a visual narrative rather than five repeated text rows. New imagery follows the work: existing architecture, conceptual exploration, specific material decisions, making, and resolved details. All five photographs are generated concepts; none depict a real Dee Creative job or workshop. The shared footer discloses illustrative imagery without overlay labels.

- Discover: a wide room study on warm paper, followed by the opening questions.
- Imagine: a portrait creative-worktable photograph against full-width warm graphite.
- Design: a measured material-and-drawing composition on soft ivory, paired with the practical planning copy.
- Build: full-width charcoal with a wide joinery photograph and execution narrative.
- Finish: a portrait of considered cabinetry and hardware on a warmer stone background.

Large image fields alternate with narrower editorial compositions. Step navigation gives a direct route to each stage. Existing scope, budget, construction, and project leadership copy remains present. Mobile keeps the logical order of stage heading, photograph, and explanation, with deliberate crops and full-width backgrounds.

Assets are generated and inspected individually, then saved in `public/images/process/`. Prompts are recorded in `docs/process-image-prompts.md`. No photographs from the rest of the site are reused on this page.
