# Process photographic assets

All five assets were generated individually with the built-in image generation tool and inspected before integration. They are conceptual process illustrations, not photographs of actual Dee Creative projects.

## discover — public/images/process/discover.png

Original photorealistic architectural editorial photograph for the Discover stage of a Portland design-build studio's Process page. Landscape 3:2. A modest older Portland dining room awaiting a thoughtful new purpose: empty alcove beside tall old sash windows, restrained original trim and honey oak floor, a single simple chair, a rolled linen curtain, natural off-white walls. No shelving, no built-in window seat, no library, no staged luxury furniture. The composition reveals room proportions, paths and soft Pacific Northwest daylight, looking carefully at the existing architecture. Quiet luminous thoughtful architecture-book photography with natural material texture and credible straight geometry. A room full of possibility rather than a dramatic before/after. No people, words, branding or watermark. No dereliction or exaggerated mansion. Earthy paper, oak, stone, subtle olive colors.

Not technical finished plans, no laptop screens, no readable text, no watermarks. Rich warm graphite background wall, muted walnut desk, soft lateral overcast light, tactile paper, generous composition, thoughtful atmosphere of exploring different ideas. Architecture-book photographic quality, restrained intentional styling, plausible modest work setting. No people or hands, no repeated existing home library or reading nook imagery. No luxury props or gold gradients.

## design — public/images/process/design.png

Original photorealistic editorial photograph for Design stage of a Portland architectural interiors studio Process page. Landscape 3:2. Top-down still life of an actual buildable interior specification in development on warm pale stone worktop: tidy graphite floorplan and cabinetry elevation with fine believable linework but no readable words or numbers, two small white-oak and smoked-oak timber samples, dark olive painted sample, a rectangular aged bronze cabinet pull, off-white plaster sample, linen swatch, architect scale and one sharpened pencil. Precisely arranged with generous negative space and quietly purposeful composition. No card model or loose perspective sketches, visually distinct from an Imagine studio photograph. Soft natural daylight with subtle shadows, tactile surfaces, paper grain, architectural editorial photography, warm ivory/olive/bronze palette, no saturated colors or glossy gold. No people, logo, watermark, gibberish text, no excess clutter. This is an imagined concept visual, not a real firm's plans.

Landscape 3:2 with important detail centered for a wide 2:1 crop. Close view of a real-sized woodworking bench and a newly fabricated oak cabinet frame: neatly fitted mortise-and-tenon corner, warm natural end grain and precise shoulder, one wooden mallet and a sharp-looking but safely resting bevel-edge chisel on bench beside curled shavings, a modest metal clamp holding the frame. No hands or people, no power-tool action, no showroom, no finished room. Focus on care in fitting and fabrication, tactile honest timber, softly blurred practical workshop background with restrained tools. Directional natural window light, rich charcoal shadows, walnut/oak/steel palette, architectural magazine photography, credible tool and joinery geometry. No decorative luxury props, fake brand logos, words, watermark or hazardous scene. Imagined process illustration, not a documented job.

Tight editorial composition celebrating the relationship of finish, hardware, light and precise craftsmanship rather than showing a whole room. Soft warm natural side light, tactile paint, stone and bronze, rich but restrained palette, no black/gold luxury hotel, no excessive decoration. Credible geometry, eye-level architectural photography, generous negative space, no people, logos, text or watermarks. Imagined finished design detail.
